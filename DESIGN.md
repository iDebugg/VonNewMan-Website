# Von Newman website design

The visual world is human, confident and technically credible. Documentary photography carries the emotional register; bold grotesk typography carries the hierarchy; product interfaces and diagrams carry the proof.

The base palette is warm white, near-black forest and pale sage, with a fresh lime accent reserved for action and emphasis. Compositions use large headlines, asymmetric editorial layouts and generous pauses. Rounded geometry is concentrated in actions, images and major framed surfaces rather than repeated card grids.

Consulting and engineering lead the narrative. Atlas and Sonar appear as immersive demonstrations of what the firm owns and can adapt. The existing dark world-map location section, live clocks and Lagos/London office structure are retained as a recognisable part of the experience.

Motion is limited to the opening image treatment, navigation feedback, tabs, disclosure controls and reduced, varied section reveals. It never competes with reading.
