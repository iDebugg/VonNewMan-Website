# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Leaders in public institutions, banks, insurers and enterprises evaluating a technology partner for software, learning, cloud, infrastructure or security work.

## Product Purpose

The website establishes Von Newman Technology Consultants as a serious consulting and engineering organisation born in Africa and operating internationally. It helps a prospective client understand the firm's capabilities, see tangible evidence in Atlas and Sonar, understand how engagements work and start a conversation.

## Positioning

Von Newman combines consulting and engineering with ownership of the products it deploys. Its teams can adapt the product, delivery model and roadmap around an institution rather than installing a fixed third-party package.

## Operating Context

The firm works across Nigeria and the United Kingdom with public and private-sector organisations. Engagements may include fixed-scope builds, managed services, licensing and team augmentation, priced in naira or sterling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site presents eight practice areas, two in-house products, public and private-sector expertise, a four-stage delivery method, leadership, offices and an enquiry form. The form currently opens the visitor's email application. The existing world-map and live-clock location experience should remain. Case-study outcomes and published thinking must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied Von Newman logo assets. The visual direction is human, bold and premium, with strong typography, documentary imagery, forest green, warm neutrals and a fresh lime accent. Von Newman Suites is the reference for warmth and presentation. The consultancy must lead the story; Atlas and Sonar support it as evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

Real product interface captures for Atlas and Sonar, four supplied organisation identities, six leadership portraits, 25 Atlas pathways, 209 Atlas courses, eight practice areas and two markets. No approved case studies, testimonials or client-outcome metrics are currently available.

## Product Principles

- Lead with consulting judgment and engineering capability.
- Show real products and delivery practices as evidence.
- Represent Lagos as the firm's home and international operation as established context.
- Keep factual claims traceable to supplied content.
- Make it easy to understand the next step and start a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, labelled controls and WCAG-aligned contrast.
